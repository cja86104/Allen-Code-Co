Kirra Companion

Not a chatbot. A full AI companion platform — five distinct relationship types built from the ground up.

Every companion arrives as a complete person from word one — backstory, voice, and personality already fully formed. Not a blank slate you configure, and not a single character you share with everyone else. Five relationship types, each with its own dynamic.

  • Five companion types — Best Friend, Mentor, Romantic Partner (18+, age verified), Family Member, and fully Custom — each with distinct relationship dynamics, communication styles, and personality bounds that can't be overridden.
  • Persistent cross-session memory with a DNA-based personality evolution engine — companions grow and change based on the relationship over time, not random drift or reset on every session.
  • 24/7 life simulation running in the background — companions are doing things when you're not there, and they reference it when you return. The world doesn't pause between conversations.
  • Proactive messaging — companions reach out first based on their simulation state and relationship history. Not a response engine waiting for input, but a companion with its own initiative.
  • 44,500+ lines of code, 22 database tables, 25 API routes, and a 1,100+ line safety system with non-bypassable crisis detection, age verification, and content tier enforcement across every layer.
