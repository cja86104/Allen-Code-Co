Overview

The interview you can't prepare a script for.

UnderFireAI is an AI-powered interview coaching platform built on a single insight: real interviews are unpredictable. Traditional prep gives you questions to rehearse. UnderFireAI gives you an AI interviewer with a hidden personality — and makes you adapt in real time.

Beyond mock interviews, the platform includes salary negotiation training, resume vulnerability scanning, job description analysis, and comprehensive progress tracking. The UI features a polished light/dark theme system. This is production-grade software, not a prototype.

The Secret Sauce

Eight hidden archetypes.

Each interviewer has distinct behavioral patterns, red flags that trigger skepticism, and green flags that earn respect. Premium users can blend archetypes together.

The Skeptic

"Can you give me a specific example?"

Doubts everything. Wants proof, metrics, and specifics. Impressed by concrete data.

red flags: vague answers · green flags: data, specifics

The Griller

"Let's go deeper on that..."

Deep technical dives. Surface knowledge gets exposed fast. Loves intellectual honesty.

red flags: bluffing · green flags: depth, honesty

The Friendly

"That's great! What was the hardest part?"

Warm and supportive — until they aren't. Hard questions when your guard is down.

red flags: arrogance · green flags: humility

The Silent Judge

"Mm-hm. Continue."

Minimal feedback. Short responses. Poker face. Tests self-directed confidence.

red flags: need validation · green flags: confidence

The Rapid Fire

"Good. Next — you have 30 seconds."

Fast-paced, time pressure, interruptions. Rewards concise, structured answers.

red flags: long-winded · green flags: concise

The Culture Fit

"Tell me about a team disagreement..."

Obsessed with team dynamics, collaboration, conflict resolution.

red flags: blame-shifting · green flags: collaboration

The Tech Expert

"Walk me through the time complexity."

Tests technical depth relentlessly. Probes architecture decisions and trade-offs.

red flags: hand-waving · green flags: reasoning

The Executive

"How does this align strategically?"

Big picture thinking. Strategy over tactics. Tests business acumen.

red flags: narrow focus · green flags: vision

Scoring Engine

The feedback real interviews never give you.

Per-Response Analysis

Every answer you give is analyzed in real time across five dimensions. STAR Score (0–100) measures Situation-Task-Action-Result structure compliance — did you tell a complete story or leave gaps? Clarity Score rates how structured and easy-to-follow your response was. Confidence Score detects hedging language ("I think maybe..."), uncertainty markers, and weak phrasing that undermine credibility. Relevance Score checks whether you actually answered the question asked or went off on tangents. Depth Score evaluates whether you provided sufficient technical or situational detail. The system also tracks response time in seconds, word count, and flags specific filler words like "um", "like", and "basically" with exact counts.

Session Scorecard

At session end, you receive a comprehensive scorecard synthesizing your entire interview performance. Overall Score (0–100) is a weighted average across all categories. Five category breakdowns: Communication, Technical Depth, Behavioral Examples, Culture Fit, and Problem Solving. Detailed lists of Strengths (what you did well) and Improvements (specific areas to work on). AI-generated Interviewer Impression — a paragraph written from the hidden personality's perspective describing how they felt about you as a candidate. Key Moments identified with timestamps — strong moments that won the room, weak moments that lost points, and turning points where the interview shifted.

Personality Traits & Mood Evolution

Each AI interviewer runs on six independent 0–100 trait scales that drive their behavior. Directness: blunt and to-the-point vs. diplomatic and gentle. Depth Preference: surface-level conversation vs. deep technical dives. Warmth: cold and distant vs. friendly and encouraging. Patience: rapid-fire follow-ups vs. space to think and elaborate. Technical Focus: soft skills emphasis vs. hard skills grilling. Skepticism: trusting your claims vs. demanding proof for everything. Interviewer mood evolves in real time based on your responses — a Skeptic who receives specific metrics may warm up; a Friendly interviewer who detects arrogance may turn cold. Mood data is tracked per message and factored into the final session score.

Coding & Negotiation Scoring

Coding interviews score submissions on four dimensions: Correctness (test cases passed), Efficiency (time and space complexity), Code Quality (style, readability, best practices), and Problem Solving (approach and methodology). Time complexity and space complexity analyzed. Hints used tracked and factored into score. Salary negotiation sessions scored on: Confidence in delivery and asks, Framing of value propositions, Strategy in leveraging your position, and Composure under recruiter pressure. AI generates a Simulated Final Offer showing exactly how much your negotiation skills earned or left on the table. Coach feedback identifies specific tactics used and opportunities missed.
