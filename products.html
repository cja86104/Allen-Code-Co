My Teachers Toolbox

Everything a K–5 teacher needs in one place — built alongside an actual teacher, used in real classrooms.

We sat with a working teacher and built what she actually wanted — lesson plans, emergency sub kits, parent communications, a full gradebook, and a vault of student data that's safe by design. Used every school day, and trusted with real student information.

  • AI builds the whole lesson on demand — plans, materials, assessments, even the note home to parents — tuned to the grade level and subject the teacher is actually teaching.
  • More than 50 emergency substitute tools. A teacher can step into any classroom and be ready in under a minute — activity packs, seating charts, behavior plans, and full resource kits generated on the spot.
  • Student data lives in a vault built to school privacy standards. Every account isolated, no cross-account exposure at any plan level.
  • Built for teams too — schools and districts share resource libraries, manage gradebooks together, and track student progress across departments.
  • More than 25 AI-powered tools across lesson design, classroom management, professional development, communication, and assessment — tiered subscription, every tool tested by a real teacher.

Kirra Companion

An AI companion platform with five distinct relationship types — built from the ground up, not a chatbot.

Every companion arrives as a complete person from word one — backstory, voice, and personality already fully formed. Not a blank slate you configure, and not the same character shared with everyone else. Five relationship types, each with its own dynamic.

  • Five distinct companion types — Best Friend, Mentor, Romantic Partner (18+, age verified), Family Member, and fully Custom — each with its own relationship dynamic and personality boundaries that can't be overridden.
  • Persistent memory across every session. The companion's personality evolves with the relationship over time — not random drift, not a reset on every conversation.
  • A 24/7 life simulation running in the background. Companions are doing things when you're not there, and they reference it when you come back.
  • Proactive messaging — companions reach out first, based on their simulation state and your shared history. Not a response engine waiting for a prompt.
  • A safety system designed in from the start — crisis detection that can't be bypassed, age verification, and content tiers enforced at every layer.
